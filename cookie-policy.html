1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how their site is being used.

There are different types of cookies:

  • First-party cookies — set by the website you are visiting directly.
  • Third-party cookies — set by a domain other than the one you are visiting (e.g., analytics or font services).
  • Session cookies — temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies, including the Google Analytics cookies, through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

You can also use browser extensions such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger to block third-party requests entirely. Note that blocking Google Fonts requests may cause the site to display in your system's default font, but this will not affect the calculator's functionality.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. When we detect either signal, Google Analytics runs without cookies, so your browser is not recognized from one visit to the next.

7. GDPR and Cookie Consent

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive, websites must obtain user consent before setting non-essential cookies. To respect this, analytics cookies are switched off by default for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, so no non-essential cookies are set there and no consent banner is shown. The local storage used by the calculator only holds results you created and your decimal-places setting, and is used solely to provide those features to you. We provide this policy for full transparency about how your browser interacts with our site and the third-party services it connects to.
